Source: Tea_Tale_Catalogue.docx supplied by the owner. The original order, names and status are recorded in data/catalogue-source.json. Complete body and tables are preserved in docs/catalogue-transcript.txt. Embedded drawings occur inside each product's detail table, with image relationships rId8 through rId23 in product order. Cover images repeat rId8 and rId9. All 16 image placements were checked against a contact sheet.

The images are promotional label artwork, not photographs. Himalayan Black Tea’s artwork says “Classic Black Tea”; document heading and range table establish the product name Himalayan Black Tea. Original names take precedence. The public crop retains that label wording honestly; replace this artwork before launch if the packaging name is changing.

Original PNG bytes are preserved under assets/originals. Full 480/960 WebP derivatives are private under assets/review. Only product-name crops are public under public/teas. This avoids publishing unapproved health, nutrition, organic and natural claims on the labels. Admin can inspect the full image through an authenticated route. Descriptive alt text identifies cropped catalogue label artwork. New approved photography can be placed under public/teas and assigned in admin.

Production seed: 16 products, two AVAILABLE with unset price and stock (Setup required), fourteen COMING_SOON. The first two packs are 100 g and 12 months best-before from packing. Other commercial fields are null. No sample inventory or prices are in the seed. Rerunning seed does not overwrite owner edits.

Owner confirmation required:

- Actual suppliers and traceable Himachal Pradesh sourcing for the two launch teas; source claims are retained privately pending confirmation.
- Darjeeling and Kangra geographic-origin usage, including the exact catalogue product name Premium Darjeeling Sweet Tea. The name is preserved as requested, but its geographic designation needs approval before launch.
- Organic / NPOP certification, “100% natural,” nutrition, antioxidant, health, medicinal and vegetarian claims. These have not been imported as public benefits.
- Ingredient lists, allergen warnings and any required herbal precautions. Traditional ingredient origins are private source context, not supplier locations. Do not infer almond or saffron ingredients from Kahwa history or serving suggestions.
- FSSAI licence, final labels, business identity/address and contact details for policies.
- Prices, opening stock, pack weights and best-before values where missing.

Public copy is a restrained adaptation of the catalogue. Product taste, caffeine, brewing and common storage guidance are imported. No certification or therapeutic assertions have been substituted with other unverified assertions.
