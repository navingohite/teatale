Good to know for every tea

How to brew the Tea Tale way

Use about 1 teaspoon (2 g) of tea for a 200 ml cup. Each product page gives its own water temperature and time.

Green tea likes cooler water (75–80°C). Black tea and herbal infusions like water close to boiling.

Strain, sip slowly and enjoy it plain, with honey or with a slice of lemon. Most of our teas can also be served iced.

Storage and freshness

Keep the tea in its airtight glass jar, in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and moisture.

Always use a dry spoon. Close the lid tightly after every use.

Best before: 12 months from the date of packing for Himalayan Green Tea and Himalayan Black Tea. Upcoming teas will state their own date on the pack.

Pack details

Himalayan Green Tea and Himalayan Black Tea: 100 g each. Weights for upcoming teas will be confirmed at launch.

All Tea Tale teas are vegetarian (green dot on the pack).

Please note

Enjoy as part of a balanced lifestyle. Our teas are not intended to diagnose, treat or cure any condition.

If you are pregnant, nursing, on regular medication or have a health condition, please check with your doctor before trying herbal teas.

Caffeine: green tea is low to moderate, black tea is moderate to high. Blue tea and chamomile are caffeine-free.
